# Welcome to Maharaj Downloader

Maharaj Downloader is a free download manager for Windows 10 and Windows 11, prepared by **Sasanka Boruah (VU3INI)** for personal use. It downloads files faster by splitting them over several connections, resumes broken downloads, catches downloads from your browser, downloads **videos and MP3 audio** from web pages, image galleries and torrents — and it works fully offline: every helper it needs is installed with it.

## What you can do

- **Download a file**: copy its link, press **Ctrl+N** (Add URL), and click **Start Download**. See [Your first download](guide-first-download.md).
- **Download a video or its sound as MP3**: use **Tasks → Download video from a page...** or paste the page's address into Add URL. See [Videos and MP3 audio](02-video-and-mp3.md).
- **Catch downloads from your browser**: add the browser extension. See [Browser integration](browser-integration.md).
- **Download overnight or a few at a time**: use queues and the Scheduler. See [Queues and the Scheduler](queues-and-scheduler.md).
- **Download torrents and magnet links**: see [Torrents](torrents.md).
- **Search for torrents**: Internet Archive's public-domain films, music and books, and your own sources (RSS, Jackett, Prowlarr). See [Search torrents](02b-torrent-search.md).
- **Switch between the light and the dark theme**: click the theme button at the bottom right of the main window, or choose **View → Light theme**, **Dark theme** or **Follow Windows theme**. See [Light and dark theme](03-themes.md).

## Finding help

- Press **F1**, click **Help** at the bottom right of the main window, or choose **Help → Help contents**.
- Type words into **Search the Help** at the top left of this window: every page is searched, the matching sections are listed with a short extract, and the words you looked for are highlighted on the page.
- **Contents** shows the full table of contents of the user guide. **Back** returns to the page you came from.
