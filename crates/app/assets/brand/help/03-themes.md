# Light and dark theme

Maharaj Downloader can be shown in a **light** or a **dark** theme, or follow Windows' own setting.

## Switch with one click

At the bottom right of the main window, click **Dark theme** (when the window is light) or **Light theme** (when it is dark). Every window of Maharaj Downloader changes at once, and the choice is remembered.

## Choose in the View menu

The **View** menu offers three choices:

- **Light theme** — always light.
- **Dark theme** — always dark.
- **Follow Windows theme** — light or dark as Windows' **Settings → Personalisation → Colours → Choose your app mode** says, changing when Windows does.

## Looks and colours

**View → Theme** changes the look of the controls and colours: Classic, Native (Windows' accent colour), High contrast, Metro, and others; each of them has a light and a dark side. You can also make your own theme in a text file. See [Themes](themes.md).

## Bigger text

**View → Interface size** makes every window larger, from 100 % to 200 % (**Ctrl +**, **Ctrl −**, **Ctrl 0**).
